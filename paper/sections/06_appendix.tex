\begin{tablenotes}[flushleft]
\scriptsize
\item The detector, the embedder and every baseline are ready-to-use checkpoints. Only the supervised typing head sees legal labels. Bootstraps are invariant across resampling seeds $42$ / $7$ / $123$ (CIs agree to ${\pm}0.1$ AMI).
